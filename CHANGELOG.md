# Changelog

## 0.1.0
First release.
- 20 Ice Age animals, each built to the real animal's shoulder height and length (the build checks every model against `src/sizes.mjs`): woolly mammoth, American mastodon, woolly rhinoceros, Elasmotherium, steppe bison, Irish elk, wild horse, Macrauchenia, Diprotodon, Glyptodon, Doedicurus, giant ground sloth (Megatherium), Megalonyx, giant beaver (Castoroides), Smilodon, Homotherium, cave lion, cave bear, short-faced bear and dire wolf.
- Ride a woolly mammoth (two seats and a chest), a woolly rhino, an Elasmotherium, an Irish elk or a wild horse.
- The Mammoth Steppe biome (Bedrock, Custom Biomes experiment), plus steppe grass, cotton grass, arctic poppy and dwarf willow in cold biomes.
- Permafrost with frozen remains: thaw them over a lit campfire and a young Ice Age animal climbs out.
- Mammoth wool, a fur hood, coat, leggings and boots, ivory and game meat.
- An unlikely herd: now and then a mammoth, a ground sloth and a sabre-toothed cat travel south together.
- Works with Realistic Hunting: organs in every model, real weights, calibres and trophies.
- Bedrock add-on and Java Edition (Fabric, Minecraft 26.3) mod, with an English and Danish website.
